# Returns & Refunds Policy

**Last updated:** August 2026

## Your Right to Return

Under UK Consumer Rights, you have **14 days** from receiving your order to return items for any reason - no questions asked.

This is called your "cooling off period" and is your legal right.

## Standard Returns (Change of Mind)

### What You Can Return
- Unworn, unused collars in original packaging
- Individual charms in original packaging
- Items that don't fit your dog
- Items you simply changed your mind about

### What You Cannot Return
- **Custom/personalized collars:** If made to your specific color/size/charm combination and you ordered the wrong specification
- **Used or worn items:** For hygiene reasons
- **Damaged packaging:** Items removed from packaging and unable to be resold

### Return Window
- **14 days** from delivery date
- We recommend returning within 7 days to ensure processing time

## How to Return an Item

### Step 1: Contact Us
Email [your email] with:
- Your order number
- Item(s) you want to return
- Reason for return (helps us improve)

### Step 2: We'll Respond
Within 1 business day, we'll send you:
- Return confirmation
- Return address
- Instructions

### Step 3: Pack Your Item
- Original packaging preferred
- Include all components (collar, charm, packaging)
- Securely packaged to prevent damage in transit

### Step 4: Ship Your Return
- **You pay return shipping** for change-of-mind returns
- Keep proof of postage
- We recommend tracked shipping (not required but safer)

**Return address:** [Your returns address]

### Step 5: We Process Your Return
- Inspected within 2 business days of arrival
- Refund issued within 14 days of receiving return
- Email confirmation sent

## Refund Method

- Refunded to **original payment method** (same card you used)
- Appears in 5-10 business days depending on your bank
- Refund includes product price
- **Original shipping is free** so nothing to refund there
- **Return shipping costs** are not refunded (you pay those)

## Faulty or Incorrect Items

Different rules apply if we made a mistake or sent a faulty item.

### What Qualifies
- **Faulty:** Defective materials, broken on arrival, doesn't work as described
- **Damaged in transit:** Arrived broken or damaged
- **Wrong item:** We sent the wrong color, size, or product
- **Not as described:** Significantly different from our website description

### What We'll Do
1. **Free return shipping:** We send you a prepaid label
2. **Fast replacement or refund:** Your choice
3. **Processing:** Within 3 business days of receiving return

### How to Report
Contact us within **48 hours of delivery** with:
- Order number
- Photos of the fault/damage/incorrect item
- Description of the issue

Email: [your email]

## Partial Returns

Bought multiple items? You can return some and keep others:
- Each item qualifies for the 14-day return separately
- Refund is for returned items only
- Process is the same

## Exchanges

We don't offer direct exchanges. To get a different size/color:

1. Return the original item (follow standard return process)
2. Place a new order for what you want
3. We'll process your refund when we receive the return

This ensures you get your new item faster than waiting for the exchange.

## Return Inspection

When we receive your return, we check:
- ✅ Returned within 14 days
- ✅ Unused and in original condition
- ✅ All components included
- ✅ Original packaging intact

If everything passes: **Full refund**

If condition issues are found:
- We'll email you with photos and options
- You can agree to partial refund, or we return item to you
- We never process disputed refunds without your approval

## Late or Lost Returns

### If Your Return Gets Lost
- Contact us with your proof of postage
- We'll investigate and work with you to resolve
- This is why we recommend tracked shipping

### If You Miss the 14-Day Window
- Contact us anyway - we may still accept it
- We're reasonable people and assess case-by-case
- Earlier than 30 days has better chance of acceptance

## Refund Issues

### Refund Not Received?
If 14 days have passed and you haven't received your refund:

1. Check your bank account (it might be pending)
2. Contact your bank (can take 5-10 days to process)
3. Still nothing? Email us at [your email]

We'll investigate and provide proof of refund from our end.

## Non-Returnable Situations

We cannot accept returns if:
- ❌ Item shows signs of use or wear
- ❌ Returned after 14 days (without our prior agreement)
- ❌ Customized item (if you ordered wrong specs)
- ❌ Missing original packaging or components
- ❌ Item damaged due to misuse

## Damaged in Transit to You

If your item arrives damaged:

1. **Don't refuse the package** - accept it so we can process claim
2. **Take photos** of packaging and damage
3. **Contact us within 48 hours** with photos
4. **We'll arrange replacement** or refund immediately
