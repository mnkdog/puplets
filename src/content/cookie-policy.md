# Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** August 2026

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, phone, tablet) when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how you use the site.

## Cookies We Use

These cookies are necessary for the website to function. You cannot opt out of these.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|------------|---------|----------|
| `cart` | Stores your shopping cart items | Session (deleted when you close browser) |
| `session` | Maintains your session while browsing | Session |

## Local Storage

We use browser local storage (similar to cookies) to:
- Remember your cart items between visits
- Store your customization choices (collar color, charms)

Local storage data stays on your device and is not sent to our servers.

## How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:
1. **Remember your cart:** So you don't lose items when you navigate away
2. **Improve your experience:** Remember your choices while shopping
3. **Security:** Prevent fraud and abuse

We **do not** use cookies to:
- Track you across other websites
- Serve targeted advertising
- Sell your data to third parties

## Managing Cookies

### In Your Browser

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

**Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies  
**Firefox:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies  
**Safari:** Preferences → Privacy → Cookies  
**Edge:** Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Options include:
- Block all cookies
- Block third-party cookies only
- Delete existing cookies
- Get warned before cookies are stored

**Warning:** Blocking essential cookies will prevent our website from working properly (you won't be able to add items to your cart or checkout).

### Mobile Devices

**iOS (Safari):** Settings → Safari → Block All Cookies  
**Android (Chrome):** Chrome app → Settings → Site settings → Cookies
