# Dog Collar Size Guide

Choosing the right size collar is important for your dog's comfort and safety. Follow this guide to find the perfect fit!

## How to Measure Your Dog's Neck

### What You'll Need
- Soft measuring tape (or string + ruler)
- Your dog
- A treat for cooperation!

### Step-by-Step

1. **Position the tape** around your dog's neck where their collar normally sits
   - This is typically the widest part of the neck
   - Just above the shoulders
   - Not too high (throat area) or too low (chest)

2. **Check the fit**
   - Tape should be snug but comfortable
   - You should fit **two fingers** between tape and neck
   - Not tight (dog can breathe easily)
   - Not loose (collar won't slip off)

3. **Note the measurement**
   - Record in centimeters or inches
   - Round up if between sizes

### Using String Instead?

If you don't have a measuring tape:
1. Wrap string around dog's neck (two-finger rule)
2. Mark where string meets
3. Lay flat and measure with a ruler

## The Two-Finger Rule

✅ **Perfect fit:** You can comfortably fit two fingers between the collar and your dog's neck

❌ **Too tight:** Can't fit two fingers, dog coughs, difficulty swallowing

❌ **Too loose:** More than two fingers, collar spins around, could slip over head

## Puppies & Growing Dogs

### Adjustable Range
Our collars are adjustable within each size range:
- XS adjusts from 20-25cm
- S adjusts from 25-35cm
- M adjusts from 35-45cm

### For Puppies
- Measure now and allow room for growth
- Recheck fit every 2-4 weeks during growth spurts
- Order next size up if your puppy is near the upper limit

### When to Size Up
Replace collar when:
- ❌ At maximum adjustment and still tight
- ❌ Can't fit two fingers underneath
- ❌ Leaving marks on fur/skin
- ❌ Dog seems uncomfortable

## Special Considerations

### Fluffy Breeds
Dogs with thick fur (Huskies, Samoyeds, Pomeranians):
- Part the fur to measure skin level
- Check fit regularly - fur can compress over time
- May need larger size than expected

### Greyhounds & Sighthounds
Narrow heads, wide necks:
- Measure the widest part of neck
- Check collar can't slip over ears
- Consider next size up for security

### Brachycephalic Breeds
Flat-faced dogs (Pugs, Bulldogs, French Bulldogs):
- Be extra careful not to measure too tight
- Leave more room for comfortable breathing
- Check regularly as these breeds can be sensitive

**Always size up.** It's safer for your dog and you can adjust tighter, but can't make a too-small collar bigger.

## Check the Fit After Purchase

When your collar arrives:

1. **Fit it on your dog**
2. **Check the two-finger rule**
3. **Watch your dog move around**
   - Collar shouldn't slide over ears
   - Shouldn't choke or restrict breathing
   - Should sit comfortably at base of neck

4. **Not quite right?**
   - See our [Returns Policy](/returns-policy.html)
   - 14-day return window
   - Exchange for different size

## Safety Reminder

⚠️ **Never leave a collar on unsupervised for extended periods** (especially during sleep or play with other dogs)

⚠️ **Check fit weekly** - dogs' necks can change with weight, fur, or growth

⚠️ **Replace damaged collars immediately** - wear and tear affects safety
