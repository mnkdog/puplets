## What Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Contact details:** Name, email address, phone number
- **Delivery information:** Shipping address
- **Payment information:** Processed securely by Stripe (we never see or store your full card details)
- **Order details:** Products purchased, customization choices (collar size, color, charms)

### Information We Collect Automatically
- **Technical data:** IP address, browser type, device information
- **Usage data:** Pages visited, time spent on site, referring website
- **Cookies:** See our Cookie Policy for details

## How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data to:

1. **Process orders:** Fulfill purchases, send confirmation emails, arrange delivery
2. **Customer service:** Respond to inquiries, handle returns/refunds
3. **Legal obligations:** Maintain records for tax and accounting purposes
4. **Improve our service:** Understand how customers use our site
5. **Marketing:** Send promotional emails (only with your consent - you can opt out anytime)

## Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process your data under:
- **Contract performance:** To fulfill your order
- **Legal obligation:** Tax records, consumer rights compliance
- **Legitimate interest:** Fraud prevention, business analytics
- **Consent:** Marketing emails (opt-in only)

## Who We Share Your Data With

We share your information only when necessary:

- **Stripe:** Payment processing (see [Stripe's Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/gb/privacy))
- **Delivery partners:** Shipping companies to deliver your order
- **Legal requirement:** If required by law, court order, or regulatory authority

We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties.

## Data Retention

- **Order records:** Kept for 7 years (UK tax law requirement)
- **Marketing consents:** Until you unsubscribe or we close the business
- **Technical logs:** Deleted after 90 days

## Your Rights (UK GDPR)

You have the right to:

1. **Access:** Request a copy of your personal data
2. **Rectification:** Correct inaccurate information
3. **Erasure:** Request deletion (subject to legal retention requirements)
4. **Restriction:** Limit how we process your data
5. **Portability:** Receive your data in a portable format
6. **Object:** Opt out of marketing or certain processing
7. **Withdraw consent:** Unsubscribe from marketing emails

To exercise these rights, contact us at [your email].

## International Transfers

We currently ship to the UK only. Your data may be transferred outside the UK/EEA when processing payments through Stripe. Stripe is certified under appropriate data protection frameworks.

## Data Security

- HTTPS encryption on our website

- Regular security reviews
- Access controls and password protection

However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
