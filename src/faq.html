Products

What are the collars made from?

Our collars are made from PVC coated nylon webbing - a durable, waterproof material that's:

  • ✅ Waterproof & weatherproof
  • ✅ Easy to clean (wipe with damp cloth)
  • ✅ Doesn't absorb odors
  • ✅ Won't crack or peel
  • ✅ Vegan-friendly
  • ✅ Available in vibrant colors that won't fade

Are the collars really waterproof?

Yes! Your dog can swim, play in puddles, get caught in rain - the collar stays waterproof and won't smell or deteriorate.

How do I clean the collar?

Super easy:

  1. Wipe with a damp cloth
  2. For stubborn dirt: mild soap + water
  3. Rinse and dry with a towel
  4. Done!

No machine washing needed (though it would survive it).

What's included with a collar?

Every collar includes:

  • The adjustable PVC coated nylon webbing collar
  • One free charm of your choice
  • Secure hardware and D-ring

Extra charms are £3.99 each.

How many charms can I add?

We recommend:

  • XS/S collars: 1-3 charms
  • M collars: 1-4 charms

Too many charms can be heavy or uncomfortable for small dogs.

Can I buy just charms?

Yes! Individual charms are £3.99 each. They slide onto our collars but may fit other slide-charm compatible collars too.

What charm options are available?

We have 20 designs:

  • Paws (white, black)
  • Shapes (heart, star, bone, crown, diamond)
  • Food (donut, pizza, taco, avocado, strawberry, watermelon)
  • Nature (rainbow, butterfly, bee, sun, moon)
  • Fun (ghost, lightning bolt)

See the full collection on our product pages.

Sizing & Fit

How do I know what size to order?

Measure your dog's neck where their collar sits:

  • Wrap a soft tape measure around their neck
  • Use the "two finger rule" (should fit two fingers between tape and neck)
  • Check our Size Guide for measurements

What if I order the wrong size?

No problem! You have 14 days to return for a different size:

  • See our Returns Policy
  • Unused collars in original packaging
  • We'll refund when we receive it
  • Place a new order for the correct size

My dog is between sizes - which should I choose?

Always size up. A slightly loose collar can be adjusted tighter, but a too-small collar can't be made bigger.

Do the collars adjust?

Yes! Each size has an adjustable range:

  • XS: 20-25cm
  • S: 25-35cm
  • M: 35-45cm

Care & Maintenance

How do I attach/remove charms?

Our charms slide on and off the collar:

  1. Unbuckle the collar
  2. Slide charm onto the collar strap
  3. Rebuckle

They stay in place when buckled but slide freely when unbuckled.

Will the colors fade?

No! PVC coated nylon webbing is colorfast and UV-resistant. Your vibrant collar stays vibrant.

How long will my collar last?

With proper care:

  • Years! PVC coated nylon webbing is incredibly durable
  • Won't crack, peel, or deteriorate like leather
  • Outlasts traditional nylon collars

Can the collar go in the washing machine?

It would survive it, but it's not necessary! Just wipe clean with a damp cloth.

Safety

Are these collars safe for puppies?

Yes, but:

  • ✅ Choose correct size (use our size guide)
  • ✅ Check fit regularly as puppy grows
  • ✅ Never leave unsupervised with collar on
  • ✅ Size up when at maximum adjustment

Is PVC coated nylon webbing safe if chewed?

PVC coated nylon webbing is non-toxic, but collars are not chew toys. If your dog chews their collar:

  • Supervise collar wearing
  • Remove when unsupervised
  • Replace if damaged

Should my dog wear the collar 24/7?

We recommend:

  • ✅ During walks and supervised time
  • ❌ Remove during sleep
  • ❌ Remove during rough play with other dogs
  • ❌ Remove if left home alone unsupervised
